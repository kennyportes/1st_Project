2024 BMW M3 CS

$124,688

4dr Sedan AWD (3.0L 6cyl Turbo)

The 2024 BMW M3 CS (Competition Sport) is a limited-edition, track-focused evolution of the M3 sedan that balances brutal performance with four-door practicality. It is powered by a specially tuned 3.0-liter twin-turbocharged inline-six engine producing 543 horsepower—a 40-hp increase over the M3 Competition—allowing it to rocket from 0 to 60 mph in a staggering 2.7 to 3.2 seconds. To achieve its aggressive "CS" status, BMW shed roughly 75 pounds through the extensive use of carbon fiber-reinforced plastic (CFRP) for the hood, front splitter, rear diffuser, and roof. Inside, the cabin is a blend of high-tech and "hardcore," featuring the massive BMW Curved Display alongside the lightweight M Carbon bucket seats and a unique center console that removes cupholders to save weight. With its standard rear-biased xDrive all-wheel-drive system and a precision-tuned chassis, the M3 CS is designed for drivers who want a surgical tool for the racetrack that can still technically carry the family to dinner.
