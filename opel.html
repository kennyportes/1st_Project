2001 Opel Speedster

$38,573

Roadster (2.2 16V 4 cyl turbocharged)

The 2004 Opel Speedster, particularly in its turbocharged "Turbo" variant, is a purist’s dream—a mid-engine, lightweight sports car built on the same aluminum chassis as the Lotus Elise. It strips away almost all modern luxuries in favor of a raw, analog driving experience, weighing just around 2,050 lbs and featuring a spartan interior with exposed metal and manual controls. The 2004 model year is defined by its 2.0-liter turbocharged engine producing 200 horsepower, which, when paired with such a featherweight body, allows for a 0 to 60 mph sprint in under 5 seconds. With its sharp, angular styling, low center of gravity, and lack of power steering, the Speedster offers surgical handling and a level of road feedback that is nearly impossible to find in contemporary cars.
