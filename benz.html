2009 Mercedes-Benz SLR McLaren

$333,333

Roadster (5.4L 8cyl)

The 2008 Mercedes-Benz SLR McLaren is a striking byproduct of a rare collaboration between Mercedes-Benz and its then-Formula 1 partner, McLaren, resulting in a "grand touring" supercar with unmatched presence. Its long, dramatic hood houses a hand-built 5.4-liter supercharged V8 that produces a thunderous 617 horsepower, exhaling through distinctive side-exit exhausts located just behind the front wheels. While its carbon-fiber monocoque and active rear "airbrake" were inspired by high-end racing tech, the SLR was designed to cross continents at 200 mph in relative comfort, featuring a five-speed automatic transmission and a luxurious, leather-clad interior. With its iconic swing-wing butterfly doors and a front-end design meant to mimic a Silver Arrows F1 car, the 2008 SLR remains a polarizing yet prestigious titan of the 2000s that commands attention for its unique blend of German engineering and British racing pedigree
