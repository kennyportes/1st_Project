2025 Porsche 911 GT3 RS

$402,500

Cope 4.0-liter Flat-six

The 2025 Porsche 911 GT3 RS is a street-legal racing weapon that prioritizes aerodynamic mastery and high-revving theater over almost everything else. At its core is a mid-mounted, naturally aspirated 4.0-liter flat-six engine that screams to a 9,000-rpm redline, producing 518 horsepower and 342 lb-ft of torque. The car is instantly recognizable by its massive, active rear wing equipped with a Formula 1-style Drag Reduction System (DRS), which, alongside a single-cooler front concept and aggressive wheel arch vents, helps generate staggering amounts of downforce for high-speed cornering. Inside, the cabin is a focused "motorsport office" featuring lightweight carbon-fiber bucket seats, a fully digital gauge cluster with a track-specific display mode, and a steering wheel bristling with dials to adjust suspension damping and traction control on the fly. By utilizing extensive carbon fiber-reinforced plastic (CFRP) and omitting a front trunk in favor of cooling efficiency, the GT3 RS remains a lightweight, surgical instrument designed to dominate the Nürburgring while still wearing a license plate.
