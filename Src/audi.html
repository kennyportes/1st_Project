2022 Audi R8

$182,800

performance 2dr Coupe (5.2L 10cyl)

A high-performance "swan song" for the legendary nameplate, marking its final production year with a focus on raw, naturally aspirated power and everyday usability. At its core is a screaming 5.2-liter V10 engine—shared with the Lamborghini Huracán—that produces between 562 and 602 horsepower depending on whether you opt for the rear-wheel drive or the iconic Quattro all-wheel-drive system. The exterior retains its timeless, low-slung silhouette characterized by aggressive LED lighting and carbon fiber "sideblades," while the interior features a driver-centric "monoposto" cockpit that replaces a traditional central screen with the sleek Audi Virtual Cockpit. Whether in its standard performance trims or the ultra-limited, track-focused GT edition, the 2023 R8 stands as a refined yet visceral masterpiece, offering a level of civility and comfort rarely found in mid-engine supercars.
