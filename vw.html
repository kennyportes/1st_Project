2025 Volkswagen Golf R

$47,873

4dr Hatchback AWD (2.0L 4cyl Turbo)

The 2025 Volkswagen Golf R represents the peak of the Mk8.5 refresh, solidifying its reputation as the ultimate "all-weather" hot hatch with a significant boost in power and technology. Its legendary 2.0-liter turbocharged four-cylinder engine has been retuned to produce 328 horsepower—a 13-hp increase—paired exclusively with a lightning-fast 7-speed DSG dual-clutch transmission following the discontinuation of the manual gearbox. The exterior receives a sharpened look with redesigned LED matrix headlights and an illuminated front VW badge, while the interior finally addresses previous criticisms by introducing a much larger, more intuitive 12.9-inch touchscreen and backlit climate sliders. Beyond the straight-line speed (0 to 60 mph in roughly 4.1 to 4.5 seconds), the Golf R retains its sophisticated 4MOTION all-wheel-drive system with rear-axle torque vectoring, allowing for a playful "Drift Mode" alongside its daily-driver civility
